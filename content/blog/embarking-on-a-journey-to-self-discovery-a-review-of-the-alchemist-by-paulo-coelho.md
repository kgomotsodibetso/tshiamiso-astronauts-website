On the 28th of January 2023, 20 members of the Tshiamiso Astronauts Book Club gathered in the Evaton West Ext 3 community hall for their first book club session of the year. The book under review was the classic novel "The Alchemist" by Paulo Coelho. The club members were excited to delve into the philosophical journey of Santiago, the shepherd boy who embarks on a quest to find his personal legend and fulfill his destiny.

"The Alchemist" was first published in 1987 and since then, it has become a timeless tale of self-discovery and the pursuit of one's destiny. The story follows Santiago, a shepherd boy who keeps having a recurring dream of a treasure waiting for him at the foot of the Egyptian tree. He seeks the advice of a gypsy and is told to go to Egypt in search of the treasure. He leaves behind his sheep, sells all he has, and sets off on a journey that will take him across the Sahara Desert and into the heart of the ancient world.

During his journey, Santiago meets a number of people who will shape his life, including a wise old man called "Melchizedek, King of Salem," and Fatima, a beautiful desert woman who Santiago falls in love with. The alchemist, who is over 200 years old, teaches Santiago about the process of turning lead into gold, both metaphorically and literally. He tells Santiago to follow his personal legend, no matter what obstacles he may face, and that the journey is more important than the destination.

Kgomotso Dibetso presented the book, giving an overview of the story and highlighting the themes that resonated with readers. The members of the book club then shared their notes, highlighting what they had learned from the book. Phoila noted that the book taught him about the importance of knowing his vision and destination in life, and about the power of destiny relationships. Thandeka learned about the importance of following one's personal legend and listening to their heart. Makibiti emphasized the importance of valuing one's journey and never underestimating their abilities.

The members of the book club agreed that "The Alchemist" is a powerful tale of self-discovery and the pursuit of one's personal legend. The book reminds readers that every journey is a treasure and that challenges and failures are not meant to break but to build us up. The club members left the session inspired and motivated to follow their own personal legends and live life to the fullest.

In conclusion, "The Alchemist" by Paulo Coelho is a classic book that continues to inspire and motivate readers all over the world. The Tshiamiso Astronauts Book Club members had a great time discussing the book and shared their notes, highlighting the lessons they had learned. The club members left the session with a newfound appreciation for the journey of life and the importance of never giving up on their dreams.

The book club members' discussion of "The Alchemist" is just one example of how this classic novel continues to touch the lives of readers everywhere. Whether you are just starting your own personal journey or have been on the road for some time, "The Alchemist" is a must-read. It's a book that reminds us to follow our hearts, to never give up on our dreams, and to always value the journey, no matter what the destination may be.
