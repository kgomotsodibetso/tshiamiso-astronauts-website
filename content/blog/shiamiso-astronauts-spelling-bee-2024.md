On 25th October 2024, Tshiamiso Astronauts had the pleasure of hosting our Spelling Bee Competition at Chief Bambatha Primary School. This exciting day brought together 40 bright learners from Grades 4 to 7 for an engaging competition aimed at promoting literacy, confidence, and the joy of learning. Joined by teachers, adjudicators, and our valued partners from the Department of Sports and Recreation, Bacha ba Dichaba, and Evaton Mall, the event truly showcased the power of community in shaping the future of education.

## Event Highlights and Champions 🏆

The Spelling Bee featured three challenging rounds—Writing, and Oral Spelling —designed to test each learner’s language skills and boost their confidence. Our participants amazed us with their skills, enthusiasm, and perseverance.

A special congratulations to our four grade winners, one each from Grades 4, 5, 6 and 7.
These young champions rose to the occasion, showing that dedication and teamwork can lead to success. We are immensely proud of all the learners who participated!

## Positive Feedback and Key Learnings

We received wonderful feedback from attendees, and we're excited to share a few highlights:

- **Engagement and Organisation**: The event was highly rated for its organisation and schedule. A few helpful suggestions, like coordinating an early briefing session with partners, will help us streamline future events.
- **Learner Enthusiasm and Performance**: Attendees were impressed by the learners' confidence and perseverance, with some suggesting that basic public speaking training for participants could further enhance their stage presence and self-assurance.
- **Facilities and Refreshments**: The venue was well-prepared, and we were glad that the refreshments kept everyone energized. We'll ensure even more access to water and additional snacks next time to cater to all guests.
## Celebrating Community Collaboration

We extend our sincere gratitude to our partners from the Department of Sports and Recreation, Bacha ba Dichaba, and Evaton Mall for their collaboration. The combined support from community stakeholders makes a significant impact, helping us create meaningful educational experiences and empowering young learners. We look forward to continuing these valuable partnerships and to expanding opportunities for future events!
## Looking Forward

At Tshiamiso Astronauts, we believe that literacy is a gateway to limitless potential. This Spelling Bee Competition reaffirmed our commitment to nurturing young minds, promoting education, and fostering a love for learning within our communities. With the insightful feedback received from this event, we are even more prepared and motivated to host similar initiatives in the future.

We’re looking forward to growing our community partnerships and creating more opportunities for young learners to thrive. Thank you to everyone who contributed to the Spelling Bee 2024!
