In South Africa, **16 December** holds profound historical significance. It is a day that represents the journey from division to unity, officially recognised as the **Day of Reconciliation**. Rooted in our country’s complex past, it once commemorated separate events for different groups—the Battle of Blood River for the Afrikaner community and the founding of Umkhonto we Sizwe, the military wing of the ANC, in the struggle against apartheid. Today, this day transcends its historical divisions to embody national healing, unity, and the shared hope of a more inclusive society.

For Tshiamiso Astronauts, the spirit of reconciliation resonates deeply with our work. Like this symbolic day, our mission focuses on bringing people together, overcoming barriers, and fostering understanding and growth within our communities. As we reflect on **2024** and our accomplishments, we recognise the importance of collaboration and unity in achieving meaningful change.

## Celebrating Our Achievements in 2024

This year has been an incredible journey of connection, empowerment, and community-driven impact. With the support of our sponsors, partners, and the vibrant spirit of the Evaton community, we’ve achieved milestones that we are proud to share. Here are some of the highlights:

### 1. Library Renovation Project

We are proud to have successfully transformed our library into a vibrant hub of knowledge and innovation. From newly added furniture and outdoor gazebos for group activities to expanded book collections featuring 156 new titles, this renovation has made the library a welcoming space for everyone. With added internet access, it now serves over 100 individuals monthly, proving the power of investing in learning and community growth.

### 2. Vaal Mall School Expo (13–16 June)

Our participation in this dynamic event allowed us to connect with over 100 community members and potential partners. The Expo was an opportunity to showcase our programmes, highlight their impact, and foster relationships that promise exciting prospects for the future.

### 3. Tshiamiso Astronauts Spelling Bee 2024

Literacy and teamwork took center stage during our Spelling Bee at Chief Bambatha Primary School on 25 October. With participants ranging from Grades 4 to 7, this event was a celebration of youth empowerment. The positive feedback and enthusiasm from both learners and attendees confirmed its success, strengthening the foundations for further collaboration and growth.
